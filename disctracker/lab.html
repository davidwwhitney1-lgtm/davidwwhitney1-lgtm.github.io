How to read the graphs

Scroll the mouse wheel to zoom in and out in time, drag to move, double-click to see the whole capture again. Point at a graph to read every value at that moment. Click a name in a graph's key to hide or show it.

Solid lines: what the sensor read. dashed: what a sensor without limits would have read (simulated throws only). orange: the pages' rebuild past the limits. Red band: the sensor maxed out.

What each graph is
  1. Force on each axis: the accelerometer, in g. x and y lie in the disc's face, z points out of its top. It stops at 16 g.
  2. Rotation on each axis: the gyroscope, in °/s. z is the spin. It stops at about 2294 °/s (382 rpm).
  3. Total force and total rotation: what the trigger looks at. A capture starts when either passes its line.
  4. Hand force: the force with the disc's own steady part taken off (its spin force and, in flight, the air). The release test needs it under its line for 25 ms; in flight, a jump over 4 g is a hit. The coloured strip under it says which part of the release test failed at each moment.
  5. Spin and speed: what the disc and the rebuild work out, against the truth.
  6. What the disc was doing: filling its history, ready, capturing, recording the flight, waiting.
