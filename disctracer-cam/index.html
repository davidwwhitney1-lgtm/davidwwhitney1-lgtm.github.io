DiscTracer Cam

A disc golf speed gun on your iPhone: lay the phone face down under the flight line, and every disc that flies over gets its speed, spin, launch angle and backhand or forehand, spoken out loud.

Setting up

  1. Put the phone face down (or on a spare disc) 2–3 steps past where you let go, right under the line the disc will fly.
  2. Point the top of the phone at your target.
  3. For spin, draw a line on the underside of the disc, from the centre to the rim (a white paint pen on dark discs).
  4. Tap Start, put the phone down and wait for the beep. Then throw. Picking the phone up pauses it.

Not ready to throw? More → Simulated throw sends a simulated camera clip through exactly the same measuring.

Questions

No spin is shown.
The camera follows a mark on the underside to measure spin. A plain underside has nothing to follow: draw a line from the centre to the rim.
A throw wasn't measured.
The disc has to cross the camera's view, roughly 1–2.5 m (3–8 ft) above the phone. Put the phone under the line the disc really flies, a little further from you if your throws pass low. Bright sun right above the phone, or a white disc against a white sky, make a disc harder to see.
The speed looks off.
The disc is the ruler, so its size matters: set your disc's diameter in the Bag (or in More → Settings). A disc 1 % too big in the settings reads about 1 % too fast.
Which iPhones?
Any iPhone that runs the app. It films at 240 frames a second where the iPhone can (120 otherwise), and drops to 120 if the phone gets hot.
Where are my throws?
On your iPhone only: in the app, and in the Files app under On My iPhone → DiscTracer Cam. More → Export makes a copy you can keep anywhere. See the privacy policy.
