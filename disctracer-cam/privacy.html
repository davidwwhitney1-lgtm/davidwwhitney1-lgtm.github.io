DiscTracer Cam: privacy policy

Effective 26 November 2026

In short: DiscTracer Cam collects nothing. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it has no server. What it records stays on your iPhone.

What the app uses, and why

  • Camera. While you are measuring, the camera watches the sky above the phone for discs. For each throw it keeps a few cropped frames of the disc, on the phone, for the replay. Nothing else is recorded.
  • Motion. The motion sensor tells whether the phone is lying still and which way is up (for the launch angle). It is used as it happens and not kept, apart from the direction of "up" saved with each throw.
  • Location. When you start a session, the phone's position marks where you threw from; when you tap Mark landing, your position marks where the disc came down, for the distance. These positions are saved with your sessions and throws, on the phone.
  • Photos. Only when you choose to open a slow-motion video: the app reads that one video.

Where your data is

Your throws, their frames, your sessions and places, your bag and your settings are stored in the app on your iPhone (and in its folder in the Files app, so your iPhone's own backups include them). The app never sends them anywhere. Export makes a file that you choose where to put. Deleting the app deletes its data.

Links

A throw's Fly it in TechDisc's simulator link opens TechDisc's website in Safari, with that throw's numbers (speed, spin and angles) in the link; TechDisc's own privacy policy applies there. The links to the DiscTracker website open in Safari too. The app sends nothing unless you tap a link.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, children included.

Changes
